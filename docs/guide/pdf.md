# PDFs

Open a PDF from [Files](vaults.md) to read it alongside your notes.
Use **Find** to search its text, or open **Outline** for bookmarks, comments and page thumbnails.
You can drag across text, even across pages, to select and copy it.

## Link a passage to a note

Select the passage, then right-click and choose **Copy Link to Selection**.
Paste the link into a [note](notes.md), for example `[[paper.pdf#page=1&selection=0,6,0,15|a useful result]]`.
Accent highlights the passage in the PDF, and clicking the highlight opens the note.
Following the link from the note returns to that passage.
The highlight lives in the note, so sharing the PDF alone will not carry it.
If you need marks in the PDF itself, choose **Export Highlights** from the page menu.

## Draw and arrange pages

Select **Drawing** in the header to open the tool ring.
It offers a pen, highlighter, eraser, line, rectangle, circle and an Adjust tool for moving or resizing strokes.
Choose a width or colour from the tool's options.
If a stylus is connected, it draws while the mouse selects text.
To draw with the mouse instead, turn on **Draw with the Mouse** in [Preferences](preferences.md).

Use the thumbnails in **Outline** to move pages by dragging them.
Their buttons insert or delete a page, and the page menu offers the same actions.
You can also drop another PDF between pages to insert its pages there.
Press `Ctrl+Z` to undo a stroke or page edit.
Accent saves these edits in the PDF and updates page links in your notes when pages move.

## Print, export and colours

Use **Print…** or **Export as PDF…** to share the PDF with your ink and note highlights.
The original PDF stays as it was.
The viewer can recolour pages to match your theme, and **Invert PDF Colours** changes one document's appearance.
You can turn PDF recolouring off in [Preferences](preferences.md).

See [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard.md) for reading and drawing keys.
On Android, [PDF reading and links](android.md) work differently.
