# Diagrams

Open a draw.io file (`.drawio`, `.dio` or `.drawio.xml`) from [Files](vaults.md) to edit it on a canvas.

## Edit the drawing

The tool ring adds rectangles, ellipses, text, connectors and images.
Select a shape to move or resize it, or use the [Properties pane](vaults.md#find-your-way) to change its appearance.
Drag a connector from one shape to another to keep them connected as you move things around.
Double-click a shape or label to edit its text, then click away to finish.
Use `Ctrl+click` to select several shapes, and `Ctrl+Z` to undo an edit.

Accent shows a dashed box for a draw.io shape it cannot draw yet.
If the diagram uses images from the web, choose **Load** in its banner to show them.

## Pages and notes

The **Outline** pane lists pages and shows their thumbnails.
Click a page to open it, or drag its thumbnail to change the order.
Use the thumbnail buttons to add or delete pages.

To show a diagram in a [note](notes.md), write `![[flow.drawio]]` for its first page.
For a named page, use `![[flow.drawio#Page name]]`.
Click the embedded drawing to open the diagram.

## Share the result

**Print…** and **Export as PDF…** include every page.
**Export as PNG…** and **Export as SVG…** use the current page.
Exports use the file's colours, even if you have inverted the canvas to read it.
See [Preferences](preferences.md) for diagram colours and the grid, or [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard.md) for canvas controls.
