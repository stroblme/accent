# Android app

Install the APK from the [Accent releases](https://github.com/stroblme/accent/releases), or use Obtainium to follow them.
The Android app works with a folder of files on your device, like a desktop [vault](vaults.md).
Choose that folder on first launch and allow file access when asked.
If you keep a vault on several devices, sync its folder with a separate app such as Syncthing.

## Find your way around

Tap **Browse** to open Search, Files, Tags, Backlinks and Command.
Swipe between them or tap their chips.
Search finds text in the vault, while Files finds names and folders.
Tap a tag in a note to see its notes in Browse.
Backlinks shows which notes link to the document you are reading.
Command includes **New Note**, **Find** and **Print…** when they apply.

## Read and write

A [note](notes.md) opens as a rendered page.
Tap **Edit** to work on its Markdown, then **Done** to read it again.
Tap a link to follow it, or tap an image to open it on its own screen.
You can pinch to zoom an image and use **Invert** if its colours are hard to read.

## Read PDFs

A PDF's bar offers **Find**, **Contents** for bookmarks and **Invert** for its colours.
Pinch to zoom, then drag to pan a zoomed page.
Long-press a word to select it, and move the handles to extend the selection.
Choose **Copy link** to paste a passage link into a note; that link makes a highlight in the PDF.
Tap a highlight to open its note, then press Back to return to the page.
Use **Print…** in Command to print a note or PDF, or choose Save as PDF in Android's print screen.

PDF drawing and page editing are currently available in the [desktop app](pdf.md).
The desktop app also edits [draw.io diagrams](diagrams.md) and provides [Git](git.md) tools.
